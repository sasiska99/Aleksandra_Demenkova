Experience

Research Assistant

Hersam Research Group, Evanston, IL | Dec 2025 to present

  • Fabricated stacked 2D heterostructure solar-cell devices from graphene, MoS₂, MoSe₂, WS₂ and WSe₂ to study ultrafast charge transfer and hot-carrier extraction.
  • Exfoliated 2D materials and identified 10+ monolayer or near-monolayer flakes per week; characterized them with AFM and Raman spectroscopy.
  • Logged results in a shared drive and updated my mentor regularly, helping the group choose candidates for the first completed device.

Co-Founder

SandStop Pro, Evanston, IL | Mar 2026 to present

  • Designed a three-component plug and filter system over 10+ iterations to keep sand out of beached sailboat hulls while preserving drainage.
  • Reached 98% particulate filtration by mass with flow comparable to an open hull.
  • Sold the product to the Northwestern Sailing Center using a model showing $16,705 in annual sand losses for a 24-boat fleet.
  • Accepted into The Garage; pursuing a patent and a manufacturing plan.

Mathematics Teaching Assistant

Jane Street AMP, New York, NY | Jun to Jul 2026

  • Taught combinatorics and problem solving to 15 students with varied backgrounds, and gave targeted help to those falling behind.
  • Ran competitive problem-solving games on probability and creative reasoning.
